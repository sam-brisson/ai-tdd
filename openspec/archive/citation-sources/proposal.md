# Citation Sources

## Why

The drink pairing AI gives recommendations, but users have no way to verify them. Without sources, the suggestions could be made up. Citing real publications builds trust and lets users learn more if they're interested.

## What Changes

Update the AI system prompt to include citations from well-known publications (Wine Folly, Food & Wine, Wine Enthusiast, etc.) when making pairing recommendations.

Example: "According to Wine Folly, Pinot Noir pairs beautifully with turkey..."

## Success Criteria

- [ ] AI includes 2 relevant citations at the end of its recommendation
- [ ] Citations reference real, well-known publications
